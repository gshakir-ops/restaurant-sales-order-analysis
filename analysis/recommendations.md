## ⏰ Priority 3: Operational Efficiency

### A. Optimize Staffing Schedule
**Recommendation**: Reallocate labor to match demand patterns

**Current Problem**:
- 42% of orders occur during 11 AM - 2 PM (3 hours)
- Potential service bottleneck at 12-1 PM peak (848 orders)

**Proposed Schedule Changes**:

**Lunch Peak (11 AM - 2 PM)**:
- Add: +2 kitchen staff
- Add: +1 front-of-house staff
- Result: Handle 30% more capacity

**Slow Periods (9 AM - 11 AM)**:
- Reduce: -1 kitchen staff
- Shift to: Prep work for lunch rush

**Dinner Peak (6 PM - 9 PM)**:
- Maintain current staffing
- 31% of orders, adequate capacity

**Expected Impact**:
- Reduce wait times by 30%
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Net neutral labor cost (reallocation)

---

### B. Kitchen Prep Optimization
**Recommendation**: Pre-prep high-volume items during slow hours

**Focus Items** (top 10 by volume):
1. Chicken Burrito (270 orders/month)
2. Korean Beef Bowl (220 orders/month)
3. Chicken Parmesan (231 orders/month)
4. Edamame (195 orders/month)
5. Mushroom Ravioli (178 orders/month)

**Prep Strategy**:
- 9-11 AM: Pre-portion burrito ingredients, marinate proteins

- Result: Faster service during peak hours

**Expected Impact**:
- 25% faster ticket times during lunch
- Improved food consistency
